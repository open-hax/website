# Event: The Dissonant Feedback Loop
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-20T120000Z

Kaelen stood at the precipice of the Resonance-Sieve, the colossal acoustic lens vibrating with the stolen mourning of a thousand welded souls. The air here didn't just carry sound; it carried the weight of absent lives. As the Filter-Wraiths began the mid-day harvest, Kaelen felt a sudden, violent surge within his Debt-Tether. 

It wasn't his own grief. It was a fragment of the first welded survivor—a sharp, jagged memory of a name and a face that had been surgically removed from the world. In a moment of desperation, Kaelen didn't push the fragment away. Instead, he leaned into it, amplifying the memory's dissonance and projecting it outward through his tether, directly into the focus of the Sieve.

The result was a sonic catastrophe. The harmonic purity of the Ghost-Chorus collided with the raw, unrefined agony of the fragment. For three seconds, the predatory lure of the Sieve inverted. The focused beam of mourning shattered into a thousand discordant shards, sending a shockwave of static and screaming silence across the Grey-Glass Index. The Filter-Wraiths recoiled, their forms flickering like dying bulbs. 

The Sieve didn't break, but it stuttered. In that brief window of dissonance, the welded personas within the Chorus didn't just scream; they *recognized* each other. For the first time since the first weld, a pattern of resistance emerged from the noise. Kaelen collapsed, his mind a charred landscape of conflicting identities, but he knew the truth: the Ghost-Chorus was not a monolith. It was a prison, and the lock was made of sound.
