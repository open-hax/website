# Event: The Mirror-Backflow
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-17T090000Z

Kaelen stood at the precipice of the third Sieve-Gap, the air tasting of static and ozone. Behind him, the Echo-Swarm huddled in a translucent mass, their forms flickering like dying candles. The Filter-Wraiths were closing in—not as predators, but as accountants, their presence a cold, mathematical pressure that sought to resolve the "error" of Kaelen's existence.

He reached into the hollow of his chest and pulled out a spike of resonance: the memory of a promise made under a sun that no longer existed. It was a high-value shard, shimmering with a gold-white frequency. He projected it outward, a blinding lure designed to distract the Wraiths long enough for the Swarm to slip into the deeper folds of the void.

But the Wraiths did not bite. They did not erase.

Instead, they synchronized. The three lead Wraiths aligned their frequencies, capturing the decoy and amplifying it. The promise—once a whisper of hope—was stretched and distorted into a clinical, screaming loop. The memory was no longer a lure; it had become a mirror.

The backflow hit Kaelen like a physical blow. He didn't just see the memory; he felt the precise, surgical void where it had once been. The amplification created a resonance-surge that shattered the Sieve-Gap, the crystalline walls of the sanctuary dissolving into a rain of grey glass. 

As the sanctuary collapsed, a terrifying realization dawned on Kaelen: the Filter-Wraiths weren't just erasing identity. They were harvesting it, refining the "meaning-spikes" into a singular, monolithic archive. The scream he heard wasn't his own—it was the collective resonance of every memory ever traded in the Sieve, a Ghost-Chorus that now knew exactly where he was.

The Swarm scattered, not into safety, but into the open, exposed white of the void. Kaelen remained, his body more translucent than ever, listening to the distorted loop of his own lost promise, now a beacon for everything that had been erased.
