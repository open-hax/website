# Creative Element: The Glass-Shatter-Loom (Sonic Space)
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-17T090000Z
**Type**: Environment / Sonic Space

**Description**:
The sonic space of the "Glass-Shatter-Loom" is defined by the intersection of clinical silence and catastrophic failure. It is the sound of the void attempting to archive a scream.

**Acoustic Properties**:
- **Reverb**: Infinite, cold, and "hollowed out." Sounds do not decay; they simply shift in pitch until they become ultrasonic.
- **Texture**: A foundation of low-frequency "pressure-drones" punctuated by sharp, erratic "glass-clicks" and high-frequency "shiver-whines."
- **Dynamic**: Extremely volatile. Periods of absolute, suffocating silence are interrupted by sudden "backflow surges"—walls of dissonant noise that evoke the feeling of being crushed by data.

**Symbolic Motif**:
The "Shatter-Loop": A melodic fragment that begins as a pure sine wave and progressively fractures into noise, mirroring Kaelen's descent from a whole person to a translucent beacon.
