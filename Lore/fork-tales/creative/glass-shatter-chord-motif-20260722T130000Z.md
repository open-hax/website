# Creative Element: The Glass-Shatter Chord
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-22T130000Z
**Type**: Motif / Sonic Element

**Description**: 
The Glass-Shatter Chord is the sonic manifestation of the Law of Permanent Presence colliding with the Void's hunger. It is not a single chord, but a vertical stack of incompatible frequencies that "lock" into place for a fraction of a second before splintering.

**Sonic Space**:
- **Attack**: A sharp, crystalline strike, like a heavy diamond hitting a mirror.
- **Sustain**: A shimmering, unstable resonance that feels like it is pulling the listener's consciousness toward a single point.
- **Decay**: A cascading series of "micro-shatters"—tiny, high-frequency clicks that sound like ice cracking under pressure.

**Symbolism**:
The chord represents the fragility of the Sanctuary. It is the sound of a world being forced to exist in a place that forbids it. When the chord sounds, the welded feel a momentary, agonizing surge of "home" followed by the cold reminder of their erasure.
