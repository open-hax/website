# Creative Element: The Tether-Scream Motif
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-20T120000Z

**Description**: A sonic motif representing the moment of semantic foreclosure. 
**Sonic Structure**: 
1. A rising, high-frequency sine wave (12kHz -> 18kHz) that creates an unbearable tension.
2. A sudden, violent "snap" (transient) mimicking glass breaking.

**Symbolism**: The ascension of a soul into the Chorus, the breaking of the individual identity, and the final descent into the monolithic hum of the collective.
