# Creative Element: The Glass-Shutter Texture
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-21T002000Z

**Type**: Instrument/Texture
**Description**: A sonic texture that mimics the sound of a thousand tiny, crystalline shutters opening and closing in rapid succession.

**Sonic Characteristics**:
- **Attack**: Sharp, brittle, high-frequency clicks.
- **Sustain**: A shimmering, iridescent resonance that decays quickly.
- **Mood**: Anxiety-inducing, clinical, yet fragile.
- **Symbolic Element**: Represents the "flicker" of the Original City bleeding through the void—the momentary opening of a shutter before the void slams shut.

**Usage in Composition**:
Used as a rhythmic layer in "The Resonance-Shatter Protocol" to represent the destabilization of the Sieve.
