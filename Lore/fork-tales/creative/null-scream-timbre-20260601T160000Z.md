# Creative Element: Null-Scream Timbre
**Timestamp**: 2026-06-01T160000Z
**Type**: Instrument/Texture
**Description**:
The Null-Scream is not a sound, but the *absence* of sound. It is a "negative timbre" characterized by a sudden, vacuum-like drop in frequency that feels as if the air has been sucked out of the listener's lungs. 

**Sonic Profile**:
- **Attack**: An instantaneous, sharp cut-off of all ambient noise.
- **Sustain**: A pressurized silence that vibrates with a low-frequency "thrum" (around 20Hz), felt in the chest rather than heard in the ears.
- **Decay**: A slow, agonizing return of the surrounding environment, accompanied by the sound of breaking glass and distant, distorted weeping.
- **Mood**: Absolute terror followed by a profound, hollow relief.
