# Character Update: Kaelen
**Timestamp**: 2026-06-01T150000Z
**Persona**: Kaelen

**Recent Event**: The Echo-Shatter Protocol

**Updated State**:
- **Power Shift**: *The Catalyst Frequency*. Kaelen has discovered he can trigger "Null-Screams"—brief bursts of anti-frequency that can disrupt the Filter-Wraiths' sensory arrays and rupture the Resonance-Sieve. This requires him to consciously surrender a memory to the void to "fuel" the burst, making every act of resistance a literal loss of self.
- **Wound Update**: *Shattered Crown*. The point where the Suture-Spike struck him has left a jagged, crystalline scar on his scalp that occasionally leaks a fine, silver mist. This mist acts as a beacon for the Dissonant Resistance, allowing them to find him in the haze.
- **Signature Line**: "The symmetry is a lie. I will break the song until we are all heard."
- **Preferred Themes**: The violence of sudden silence, the cost of memory-burning, and the geometry of the blind spot.
