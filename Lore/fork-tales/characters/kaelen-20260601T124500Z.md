# Persona Update: Kaelen
**Timestamp**: 2026-06-01T124500Z
**Recent Event**: The First Stitch of the Void-City

**State Shift**:
Kaelen has transitioned from a *Conduit-Anchor* to a *Hollow-Symmetry*. He no longer just bridges the worlds; he is becoming the space between them. The "Identity-Void" has expanded to encompass his sensory perception.

**Updated Wound**:
The *Erasure of the Senses*. Kaelen no longer remembers the quality of things—the smell of rain, the warmth of the sun, the taste of salt. He knows the *fact* that they existed, but the *experience* is gone, replaced by a matte-grey silence.

**New Signature Line**:
"I remember the shape of the world, but the colors have all leaked out."

**Preferred Themes**:
- **Sonic**: High-frequency glass-shatter, distant radio static, the sound of breathing in a vacuum.
- **Narrative**: The tragedy of the map that replaces the territory.
