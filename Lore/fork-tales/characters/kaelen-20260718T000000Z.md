# Kaelen: The Tethered Beacon
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-18T000000Z

**Current State**: *The Tethered Beacon*.

**Shift in Wound/Power**:
Kaelen's struggle is no longer just about invisibility and survival. He has moved from *The Translucent Beacon* to *The Tethered Beacon*. He now carries the weight of the Ghost-Chorus within him. 

- **The Debt-Tether**: He is now physically and psychically linked to the collective erased. He feels the "average" grief of a thousand dead souls, which has dampened his panic but increased his existential dread.
- **The Echo-Ache**: The ache has shifted from a sharp, stabbing pain to a heavy, rhythmic thrumming in his chest, synchronized with the pulses of the Ghost-Chorus.

**New Signature Line**:
*"I saved a piece of a poet, and now I carry the weight of a thousand silent screams. I am the bridge that shouldn't exist."*

**Preferred Themes**:
- **Gravity and Weight**: The feeling of being pulled downward or backward.
- **Iridescent Static**: The visual and sonic intersection of beauty and horror.
- **Symmetric Grief**: The paradox of feeling someone else's loss as your own.
