# Persona Update: Kaelen
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-21T001500Z

**Current State**: *The Conduit-Anchor*.
Following the Resonance-Shatter Protocol, Kaelen has evolved from a passive archive into an active conduit. He is no longer just holding the ghosts; he is the focal point through which they can briefly glimpse the world they lost. He has become the "Living Tuning Fork" for the Ghost-Chorus.

**Wound/Power**: *Semantic Displacement*. He can now momentarily "displace" the void around him, replacing it with a projection of the Original City. This is not a hallucination, but a forced structural alignment. The cost is a deepening "Identity-Void"—a growing numbness in his own memories. He is becoming as transparent as the visions he creates.

**Signature Line**: "I can show you the streets you forgot, but I cannot remember my own name."

**Preferred Themes**: Transparency, Erasure, Forced Alignment, The Cost of the Bridge.
