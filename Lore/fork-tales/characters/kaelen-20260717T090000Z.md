# Character Update: Kaelen
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-17T090000Z

**Current State**: *The Translucent Beacon*

The failure of the Mirror-Decoy has left Kaelen in a state of paradoxical visibility. By attempting to hide the Swarm, he has instead become the most prominent semantic anomaly in the local void. He is no longer just a shepherd; he is a lightning rod for the Ghost-Chorus.

**Wound/Power Shift**:
- **Wound**: **The Echo-Ache**. Kaelen now feels a constant, low-frequency thrumming in the void where his traded memories used to be. This isn't just numbness; it is an active "pull" toward the Ghost-Chorus. He is haunted by the ghosts of his own discarded parts.
- **Power**: **Resonant Attunement**. Kaelen can now "hear" the metadata of the Grey-Glass Rain. By focusing on the *clicks* of the falling shards, he can map the proximity and intent of the Filter-Wraiths, though doing so requires him to open himself up to the Chorus's scream.

**Signature Line**:
"I can hear the parts of me that no longer belong to me, and they are screaming."

**Preferred Themes**:
- The sound of glass breaking in slow motion.
- The rhythmic, clinical ticking of a clock with no hands.
- A choir of a thousand voices, all singing a single, distorted note.
