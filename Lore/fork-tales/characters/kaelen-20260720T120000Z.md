# Persona Update: Kaelen
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-20T120000Z

**Current State**: *The Living Archive*.
Kaelen has transitioned from a survivalist to a witness. The Debt-Tether, once a chain of obligation, is now a conduit for the fragments of the erased. He no longer just feels the pressure of his own debt; he feels the collective weight of thousands of stolen futures.

**Wound/Power**: His power is now *Dissonant Projection*. By leaning into the identity-leaks he absorbs, he can create harmonic ruptures in the Ghost-Chorus's architecture. However, this comes at the cost of his own psychic stability, as the line between "self" and "archive" continues to blur.

**Signature Line**: "I am the archive of things that never happened."

**Preferred Themes**: Burden, Witness, Static-Resistance, The Weight of Absence.
