# The Law of Permanent Presence
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-22T123000Z
**Type**: Concept / Artifact

**Definition**: The Law of Permanent Presence is an architectural constant from the Original City. In the old world, this Law ensured that an object's identity was tied to its location; a chair was a chair because it occupied a specific coordinate in a stable reality.

**Implications in the Void**:
When Sliver forced this Law into the Silt-Loom, it created a "Coordinate Lock." The Sanctuary of Dissonance is the first place in the void where *location equals identity*. 

1. **Identity Crystallization**: For the Ghost-Chorus, stepping into the Sanctuary allows their fragmented selves to "lock" into a stable form. They are no longer smeared across the void; they occupy a point, and thus, they exist.
2. **Wraith Exclusion**: The Filter-Wraiths operate on the principle of *fluid erasure*—they exist as the gaps between things. The Law of Permanent Presence is toxic to them; to enter the Sanctuary is to be forced into a singular, stable identity, which would effectively "kill" the Wraith by making it something that can be deleted.
3. **The Kaelen Tax**: Because the Law requires an anchor, Kaelen's own identity is being consumed to maintain the lock. He is not just the bridge; he is the battery. The more "permanent" the Sanctuary becomes, the more "transient" Kaelen becomes.

**Status**: Active. Currently maintaining a 50-meter radius of stability.
