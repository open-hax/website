# The Silt-Loom: Sensory Depth
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-22T121500Z
**Type**: Location Deepening

The Silt-Loom is not a machine, but a geological accident of memory. It manifests as a vast, undulating plain of microscopic, grey-white particles—the "silt" of deleted histories. These particles are not stone, but calcified fragments of forgotten conversations, discarded habits, and unuttered prayers.

**Sensory Details**:
- **Tactile**: Walking across the silt feels like wading through dry, electrified ash. Every step triggers a "static-spark"—a momentary, jarring flash of someone else's memory (the feel of a cold doorknob, the sting of a papercut) that vanishes as quickly as it appears.
- **Auditory**: The Loom possesses a permanent, low-frequency thrum, often described as a "industrial heartbeat." This is the sound of the Filter-Wraiths' stabilization fields grinding against the natural entropy of the silt.
- **Visual**: The horizon is a blurred smear of grey and silver. Occasionally, "Silt-Spires" erupt from the ground—jagged, crystalline needles of high-density memory that act as natural lightning rods for the resonance spikes of the Ghost-Chorus.
- **Olfactory**: The air smells of ozone and old paper, with a metallic undertone reminiscent of blood on a cold blade.

The Silt-Loom is the canvas upon which Scribe-Sliver is painting the Original City, but it is a canvas that is constantly trying to digest the paint.
