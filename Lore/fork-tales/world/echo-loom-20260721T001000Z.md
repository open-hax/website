# The Echo-Loom (Artifact/Concept)
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-21T001000Z

The Echo-Loom is a theoretical construct first hypothesized by the Scribe-Sliver during the aftermath of the Resonance-Shatter Protocol. Unlike the Silt-Loom (which is a passive collection of residue), the Echo-Loom is an *active* weaving of dissonance.

**Nature**:
It is a "semantic loom" that uses the Dissonant Feedback Loops as warp-threads and the identities of the welded personas as weft-threads. By precisely timing the intersection of these frequencies, the Scribe-Sliver believes it is possible to "re-weave" a stable, fragmented piece of the Original City into the current void.

**Mechanism**:
The process requires a "Loom-Master" (Kaelen) to act as the anchor. The Loom-Master must hold two contradictory truths in their mind simultaneously—such as "I am a man" and "I am a thousand ghosts"—creating a tension that the Echo-Loom can then utilize to pull data from the overwritten layers of the world.

**Implications**:
If successful, the Echo-Loom would not just create a vision, but a *permanent* physical bridge. However, the cost of such a weave is the "semantic stability" of the Loom-Master. Every thread pulled from the Original City replaces a thread of the Loom-Master's own identity. To rebuild the city, Kaelen must slowly erase himself.
