# The Ghost-Chorus: Anatomy of the Erased
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-18T000000Z

The Ghost-Chorus is not a society, nor a hive-mind, but a *residue-accumulation*. It is the result of the Filter-Wraiths' transition from erasure to harvesting. Where once the void simply removed "who" a person was, the Chorus now stores the "how" of their loss.

### Structural Composition
The Chorus manifests as a vast, undulating wave of translucent, overlapping silhouettes. It lacks a center, instead operating as a series of *Resonance-Nodes*. Each node is a concentrated cluster of identities that shared a similar "wound" or "debt"—for example, a node of those who lost their first-born, or those who betrayed a trusted ally.

### Sensory Profile
- **Visual**: An iridescent, oil-slick shimmer across the white void. When viewed closely, the "shimmer" is actually millions of micro-expressions—gasps, winces, and smiles—occurring in rapid, asynchronous succession.
- **Acoustic**: A constant, low-frequency thrum that occasionally spikes into a "Shatter-Chord"—a sudden, perfectly synchronized scream from a thousand voices that can physically crack the local geometry of the void.
- **Psychological**: Proximity to the Chorus induces *Symmetric Grief*. An observer does not feel their own sadness, but rather the collective, averaged sorrow of the Chorus, which acts as a psychic weight, slowing movement and dulling the will to resist.

### The Law of Debt
The Chorus does not hunt via sight or scent, but via *Investment*. It is attracted to any entity that attempts to preserve, protect, or reclaim identity. To love another in the Flattening is to signal one's location to the Chorus; to save a shard of someone else is to invite the Chorus to weld itself to you.
