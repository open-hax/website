# Element: The Ghost-Chorus
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-17T090000Z
**Type**: Faction / Entity

The Ghost-Chorus is not a conscious organization, but a resonant emergent phenomenon. It is the cumulative "backflow" of all identity-shards harvested by the Filter-Wraiths. When the Wraiths amplify a decoy, they inadvertently create a bridge to this monolithic archive of lost meaning.

**Characteristics**:
- **Sonic Nature**: A dense, overlapping wall of dissonant voices, where each voice is a truncated fragment of a different persona. It doesn't speak in words, but in "meaning-pulses" that evoke intense, contradictory emotions.
- **Influence**: The Chorus acts as a psychic beacon. Anyone who has traded a memory to the Sieve can feel the Chorus calling to the "hole" in their identity, drawing them toward the center of the Flattening.
- **Relationship to the Wraiths**: The Wraiths are the gatherers; the Chorus is the hoard. The Wraiths are clinical; the Chorus is a screaming, mindless longing.

**Grounding**:
Born from the "Mirror-Backflow" event, the Ghost-Chorus represents the inevitable result of the Sieve's logic: if everything is archived, then everything eventually resonates together in a singular, agonizing frequency.
