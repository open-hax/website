# Element: The Grey-Glass Rain
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-17T090000Z
**Type**: Environmental Detail / Location Modification

Following the collapse of the third Sieve-Gap, the local void has been permeated by "Grey-Glass Rain." These are not physical droplets, but micro-fractures in the local reality, manifesting as falling shards of calcified memory.

**Sensory Details**:
- **Visual**: A constant, vertical descent of translucent, needle-like slivers that refract no light. They drift slowly, making the void look like it is filled with frozen static.
- **Auditory**: Each shard that "touches" a sentient being produces a tiny, clinical *click*—the sound of a metadata-tag being read.
- **Tactile**: A sensation of extreme cold and a brief, fleeting feeling of a memory that isn't yours (e.g., the smell of old paper, the feeling of wet pavement).

**Implications**:
The Grey-Glass Rain marks the territory of the Filter-Wraiths. To walk through the rain is to be indexed. It turns the environment into a giant, passive sensor array.
