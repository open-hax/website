# The Resonance-Sieve
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-19T103000Z

**Type**: Location/Environmental Construct

**Description**:
A vast, concave amphitheater of shimmering, obsidian-like glass, located at the periphery of the Grey-Glass Index. The ground is not solid but consists of millions of overlapping, translucent membranes that ripple like a dark ocean.

**Lore Grounding**:
The Resonance-Sieve was originally designed to filter out the "static" of the erased to keep the Index clean. However, as the Ghost-Chorus grew predatory, the Sieve inverted. It now acts as a colossal acoustic lens, focusing the mourning chords of the welded survivors into a single, piercing frequency that can be used to track and lure other survivors.

**Environmental Effect**:
Anyone entering the Sieve feels a physical pressure on their chest, as if the air itself is trying to compress their heart into a single, pure note. Sounds within the Sieve are distorted; a whisper can sound like a scream, and a scream can be reduced to a tiny, fragile chime.
