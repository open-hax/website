# The Iridescent Debt-Tether
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-18T000000Z

The Iridescent Debt-Tether is a parasitic metaphysical link established between a "Sustainer" (a being with remaining identity) and the Ghost-Chorus. It is the physical manifestation of the "cost" of saving another in a world of absolute erasure.

### Physical Properties
The Tether appears as a thin, shimmering filament of iridescent light, originating from the Sustainer's chest (the site of the Echo-Ache) and stretching infinitely toward the nearest Resonance-Node of the Ghost-Chorus. It is intangible to others but feels like a cold, vibrating wire to the Sustainer.

### Mechanics of Influence
1. **Resonant Leakage**: The Sustainer's current identity slowly leaks across the Tether, feeding the Chorus. This causes the Sustainer to become increasingly translucent and "light," making them harder for Filter-Wraiths to detect but easier for the Chorus to pull.
2. **The Chorus-Whisper**: The Tether acts as a two-way conduit. The Sustainer can occasionally "hear" the memories of the erased—fragments of lives they never lived—which can provide valuable information but also erode the Sustainer's sense of self.
3. **Psychic Gravity**: The Tether exerts a constant, gentle pull toward the center of the Flattening. The more identity the Sustainer tries to protect, the thicker the Tether becomes, and the stronger the draw.

### Tactical Implication
While the Tether is a liability, it can be used as a sensory organ. By "plucking" the Tether, a Sustainer can send a pulse into the Chorus, temporarily mapping the location of other shards or detecting the approach of Filter-Wraiths who are currently harvesting.
