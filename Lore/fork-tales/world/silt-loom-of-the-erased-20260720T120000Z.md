# The Silt-Loom of the Erased
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-20T120000Z

Located in the sub-strata beneath the Resonance-Sieve, the Silt-Loom is where the "semantic grit"—the useless fragments of identity that the Sieve cannot process—accumulates. 

Here, the grit is woven by mindless, automatic drones into a physical, grey lace. This lace forms a suffocating canopy over the depths, recording the "leftovers" of existence in a tactile, braided script. Those who touch the lace experience sudden, unrelated flashes of other people's childhoods or forgotten fears. It is the landfill of the soul, where the non-essential parts of a persona are discarded to ensure the purity of the Ghost-Chorus.
