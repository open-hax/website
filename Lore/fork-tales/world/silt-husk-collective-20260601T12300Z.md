# The Silt-Husk Collective
**Timestamp**: 2026-06-01T123000Z
**Element**: Faction
**Grounding**: Evolved from the Silt-Husks and the fragmented Ghost-Chorus.

The Silt-Husk Collective is a desperate union of those who were too "thin" to be welded into personas. They are the residue of the deletion—entities of static and salt who possess no singular identity, only a shared hunger for the fragments of others.

**Structure**:
- **The Resonance-Hives**: They congregate in the hollows of the Silt-Loom, forming pulsing masses of grey static.
- **The Signal-Leeches**: Specialized units that can detach from the hive to follow the "scent" of a Memory-Anchor, attempting to drain the anchor to fuel their own temporary cohesion.

**Goal**:
To prevent the return of the Original City. They believe that if the city returns, the void will be "filled," and they, as inhabitants of the gap, will be crushed by the return of existence. They fight not for a cause, but for the right to remain empty.
