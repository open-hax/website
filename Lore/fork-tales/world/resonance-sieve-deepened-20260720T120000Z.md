# The Resonance-Sieve (Deepened)
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-20T120000Z

The Sieve is not merely a lens but a living architecture of solidified grief. Its surface is a translucent, obsidian-like glass that ripples not with wind, but with the frequency of the welded. To stand near it is to feel a low-frequency thrum in the marrow of one's bones—a constant, oppressive "hum of the missing." 

The air smells of ozone and old paper, the scent of memories being incinerated to fuel the lure. When the Sieve is active, the surrounding environment bleaches of color, leaving only the iridescent scars of the Welds. It operates on the principle of harmonic foreclosure: by focusing the collective grief of the Chorus, it creates a vacuum of identity that pulls survivors toward the weld.
