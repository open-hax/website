# The Memory-Anchor Architecture
**Timestamp**: 2026-06-01T121500Z
**Element**: Location/Mechanism
**Grounding**: Linked to the Echo-Loom and the process of Manifestation via Sacrifice.

The Memory-Anchor is not a physical object, but a spatial-mnemonic parasite. It is the "weight" that allows a manifested object from the Original City to remain in the void without being immediately reclaimed by the Filter-Wraiths.

**Sensory Details**:
- **Visual**: Appears as a series of translucent, obsidian-colored chains that spiral downward from the manifested object into the void-floor. The chains pulse with a dim, rhythmic light—the "heartbeat" of the memory that fuels it.
- **Auditory**: Emits a low-frequency thrum, similar to a distant transformer, overlaid with the faint, distorted sound of the memory being used (e.g., if the anchor is a memory of rain, the thrum is accompanied by a ghostly pitter-patter).
- **Tactile**: Touching an anchor feels like a sudden drop in temperature and a sharp, momentary flash of a stranger's emotion—usually grief or longing.

**Implications**:
The anchor creates a "Stability Zone" around the manifestation. Within this zone, the laws of the void are slightly suspended, allowing other welded personas to experience a flicker of their original identities. However, the anchor is hungry; it slowly drains the remaining mnemonic residues from anyone who stays within its radius for too long, effectively "bleaching" them to keep the manifestation solid.
