# The Suture-Spike (Deepened)
**Timestamp**: 2026-07-19T101500Z

**Nature**: An instrument of metaphysical anchoring and identity-harvesting.

**Sensory Details**:
- **Visual**: A needle-thin sliver of iridescent glass that seems to exist in three dimensions and a half-dimension simultaneously. It does not reflect light; it refracts *intent*.
- **Auditory**: When dormant, it emits a low, barely audible hum—the sound of a thousand held breaths. When activated (the Weld), it produces a crystalline "snap" followed by a sustained, mourning chord.
- **Tactile**: To the touch, it is colder than absolute zero, drawing heat not from the skin, but from the memory of warmth.

**Mechanical Implications**:
The Suture-Spike does not kill in the biological sense. Instead, it performs a *semantic foreclosure*. It identifies the core "chord" of a persona's identity and welds it to a Resonance-Node of the Ghost-Chorus. The victim remains aware but loses agency, becoming a permanent harmonic layer in the collective grief. 

The spike acts as a conduit; as it welds the survivor, it siphons the "excess" identity—the specific, non-essential memories—into the Ghost-Chorus's general reservoir, increasing the predatory gravitational pull of the collective.
